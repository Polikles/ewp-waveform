## 2. Free Use

The Software may be used without payment for:

- noncommercial personal, educational, research, hobby, experimental, and
  community use;
- ordinary noncommercial teaching and classroom use; and
- commercial use by an eligible individual or Entity whose gross Revenue in the
  Previous Financial Year did not exceed EUR 25,000.

Eligibility is based on the Revenue of the person or Entity actually using the
Software, not on the size or Revenue of its customer.

For example, a freelancer with EUR 15,000 of qualifying annual Revenue may use
`ewp-waveform` for a EUR 2,000 contract for a much larger customer without
needing a separate commercial license.

## 3. Revenue Threshold

The free commercial-use threshold is:

**EUR 25,000 in gross Revenue during the Previous Financial Year.**

Revenue means total gross revenue from all sources, not merely revenue generated
with `ewp-waveform`.

Related Entities under common control are aggregated.

Revenue in another currency may be converted using a reasonable, publicly
available exchange rate applied in good faith. The project is not intended to
turn insignificant exchange-rate differences into licensing disputes.

If an eligible user exceeds the threshold during the current year, that does not
automatically terminate eligibility during that year. The change affects
eligibility for the following financial year.

A newly created Entity that has no Previous Financial Year may rely on a
reasonable good-faith expectation that its first-year Revenue will remain below
the threshold.

## 6. Tutorials About ewp-waveform

A special exception permits commercial or monetized tutorials, courses, videos,
articles, streams, workshops, books, and similar educational material primarily
teaching **how to use `ewp-waveform`**, even when the creator exceeds the
EUR 25,000 Revenue Threshold.

The exception applies only when `ewp-waveform` is the sole software product
being taught or is clearly the principal software product that is the subject of
the material.

A broader paid audio/video-production course that merely uses `ewp-waveform` as
one tool among many remains subject to the normal Revenue Threshold.

The tutorial exception does not permit selling or commercially redistributing
the Software or a modified fork.
